## Detailed description

A tiny game center that sits under your toolbar icon. Click it whenever you have a spare minute, pick something you feel like playing, and go.

The collection covers the classics — think Snake, 2048, a proper Minesweeper, a rated chess ladder — plus a few modern picks like a neon fighting game and a bluffing card game you can play against real people. There's something for a thirty-second break and something for a longer sit-down.

A few games work completely offline, so you can keep playing on a plane or when the Wi-Fi drops.

A small "Hot" badge lights up on whatever games are getting the most play this week, so you always have a hint of what's worth trying next.

No ads. No sign-up. The extension only does anything when you open it — it doesn't watch what you're doing on other tabs or ask for permission to touch your browsing history.

Made in New Zealand.
